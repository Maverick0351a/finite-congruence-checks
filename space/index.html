02 / The bounded experiment

Graph-search results for all eight targets

The search recorded 143,685 candidate colorings, 217 witnesses and 22 classes under vertex relabeling with lattice colors fixed. A witness satisfies all five conditions of the finite graph criterion. These counts describe colored graphs, not the Atlas's 313 labeled grouping families, 39 relabeling classes or 22 abstract order shapes.

M4 has no witness within the tested bound of four points. Larger carriers remain outside this experiment. Operation minima fix the saved carrier and exact relations.
